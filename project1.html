MOVIE DETAIL

PICTURE

The film adaptation of Fantasy cinema was released in 2001 under the masterful direction of Chris Columbus, earning widespread critical acclaim and an impressive movie rating of 8.1/10 on IMDb.

DESCRIPTION

An orphaned 11-year-old boy named Harry Potter discovers he is actually a wizard and has been accepted into Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. He escapes his cruel aunt and uncle to enter a magical world where he is already famous for surviving a dark wizard's attack as a baby.At school, Harry quickly makes friends with classmates Ron Weasley and Hermione Granger. Together, the trio uncovers a secret: a legendary object called the Philosopher's Stone, which grants immortality, is hidden inside the castle. Believing someone is trying to steal it for the dark lord Voldemort, the three friends must bypass dangerous magical traps to protect the stone and save the wizarding world.
