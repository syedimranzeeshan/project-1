The Aga Khan University Hospital (AKUH), established in 1985 in Karachi, is Pakistan's premier private, not-for-profit teaching hospital and primary academic medical center for the Aga Khan University.


Historic Milestone
The Aga Khan University Hospital (AKUH) opened its doors in 1985 in Karachi. It was founded by His Highness the Aga Khan to bring world-class healthcare to South Asia and serve as the main teaching hub for the Aga Khan University.
Massive Outreach Network

The healthcare network reaches far beyond the main Stadium Road campus. It manages multiple secondary hospitals, over 19 regional medical centres, and hundreds of specimen collection units across Pakistan.
